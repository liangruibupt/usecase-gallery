Shipping & delivery

Every order leaves the atelier within one business day, wrapped in black tissue and sealed by hand.

  • US standard, 3–5 business days. Included on orders over $150, otherwise $8.
  • US express, 2 business days, $18.
  • International, 5–10 business days. Duties are calculated at checkout, so nothing is due on arrival.

Fragrance is alcohol-based and travels by ground, so some remote addresses may take a day or two longer.

Returns & exchanges

Each 50 ml bottle arrives with two 2 ml samples of Fig & Smoke. Wear a sample first. If it isn't yours, return the bottle still sealed within 30 days for a full refund.

  • Return labels are free within the US.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment within 5 business days of arrival.
  • For hygiene reasons, opened bottles can't be returned unless they arrive damaged.
Ingredients & care

Alcohol denat., parfum (fragrance), aqua (water), limonene, linalool, coumarin, citral.

Keep the bottle away from light and heat, cap closed. Birch tar darkens the juice gently over time; this is natural and doesn't change the scent.

The composition

Three movements, one slow burn

Move across the pyramid to follow the scent from the first spray to the last trace on skin.

Top notes

First 30 minutes

Green fig, torn open

Not the jammy fruit but the whole tree: sap, leaf and the bitter milk at the stem, lifted by cold-pressed bergamot.

  • Green figLeaf & milk accord
  • BergamotCalabria, cold-pressed

Heart notes

From the first hour

Powdered iris, pale cedar

Orris butter, cool and almost suede-like, settles over dry cedarwood. The fig softens into something you lean toward.

  • IrisOrris butter, Tuscany
  • CedarVirginia cedarwood

Base notes

Into the night

Smoke on warm skin

Rectified birch tar brings the smoke of a cold hearth; ambrette seed gives it a musky, living warmth that stays close.

  • Birch tarRectified, Finland
  • AmbretteSeed absolute

How it wears

From first spray to the last trace

Fig & Smoke changes as it warms. Here is what to expect over an evening.

  1. The opening

    Green and bright

    Crushed fig leaf and bergamot, sharp and a little bitter, with a first breath of smoke beneath.

    Green fig · Bergamot

    ProjectionBold

  2. The heart

    Powder and wood

    Iris rises, cool and suede-soft. Cedar dries the fig into something closer to skin than fruit.

    Iris · Cedar · Fig

    ProjectionModerate

  3. The drydown

    Smoke on skin

    Birch tar and ambrette, warm and close. Noticed only by someone near enough to ask.

    Birch tar · Ambrette

    ProjectionClose

Lasts 8–10 hours on skin · longer on wool and cashmere

The flacon

Heavy glass, a cap of solid brass

The bottle is cut from a single block of glass with a thick base that holds the light. The name is engraved and frosted by hand rather than printed, so it never wears away.

The cap is turned from solid brass and knurled so it grips under the thumb. It closes with a weight you can hear. The box carries no ink at all: the logo is pressed into the black board and only shows when the light catches it.
