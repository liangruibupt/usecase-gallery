## 1. Summary of Changes

| # | Issue found | Change made | Main locations |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Trigger, invoicing and due date were combined. There were no rules for early, late or unsent invoices, or for non-Business Day due dates. | Three-stage model: Payment Trigger, then Invoice Date, then Due Date. Added rules on early invoices, invoice issue, invoice sending, non-Business Days and invoice content. | Agt 9.3; PS s.1, s.3 |
| R2 | All four Deliverables were subject to formal acceptance. | D1 and D2 are now Interim Deliverables (comment only). D3 and the D4 Materials are Final Deliverables (formal acceptance). | Agt 7.1–7.16; Annex 2 Forms A and B |
| R3 | Removing acceptance of D1 would have removed the scope baseline. | The Approved Research Plan is now fixed by a comment-and-finalise process with deemed approval. | Agt 7.5(a), 7.8; Form A |
| R4 | The long-stop ran from D3 only. | The long-stop now runs from the later of the Delivery Dates of the Accepted D3 and the Accepted D4 Materials. | PS 4.2 |
| R5 | "Reasons attributable to the Client" was a subjective test. | Objective test: the Provider offers dates; the long-stop applies unless there has been a Provider Presentation Failure. | Agt 7.17(c)–(g); PS 4.3–4.5; Form C |
| R6 | The address for rejection notices was ambiguous. | Rejections must go to the project email with a copy to the notice email. A mechanism for responding to invalid rejections has been added. | Agt 7.12(d), 7.13; Form B |
| R7 | The invoice dispute window could be used to reopen acceptance. | Invoice disputes on excluded grounds, or about Accepted Final Deliverables, are barred. | Agt 9.11 |
| R8 | Clauses 5.5(b) and 19 said "invoice immediately" without a defined trigger. | Each now names its own Payment Trigger. | Agt 5.5(b), 19.1–19.3 |
