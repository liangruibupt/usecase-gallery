# FORM A: INTERIM DELIVERABLE COMMENT FORM (D1 and D2)

## Guidance Notes for Form A

These notes summarise clauses 7.3 to 7.8 of the Agreement. If they conflict, the Agreement prevails.

1. **No acceptance or rejection.** Interim Deliverables are not accepted or rejected, and no payment depends on them (clause 7.4(a)).

2. **Comment Period.** The Client may give written Comments within [NEGOTIABLE: 5] Business Days after the Delivery Date (clause 7.3). If no Comments are received by then, the Client is treated as having none (clause 7.4(b)).

3. **Where to send Comments.** Comments are not formal notices. Send them to the Provider's project email address (clause 7.4(d)).

4. **What the Provider does with Comments.** The Provider considers all in-time Comments in good faith (clause 7.5).
   - **D1:** the Provider issues a Finalised Research Plan within [NEGOTIABLE: 3] Business Days after the Comment Period ends. It completes Part A3 to show which Comments were adopted.
   - **D2:** adopted Comments are reflected in D3. D2 is not reissued.

5. **Comments the Provider need not adopt (clause 7.6):**
   - Comments outside the scope of the SOW or the Approved Research Plan. These may be handled as a Change.
   - Disagreement with findings or conclusions reached in the Provider's professional judgement.
   - Requests to present findings in a way the evidence does not support.
   - Comments received after the Comment Period. Rework they require is a Change (clause 7.7).

6. **Approval of the research plan (D1 only; clause 7.8).** The Approved Research Plan is fixed on the earliest of:
   - the Client's written confirmation (Part A4);
   - the end of the Comment Period, if no Comments are received; and
   - [NEGOTIABLE: 2] Business Days after the Finalised Research Plan is issued. Within that window the Client may notify the Provider that an adopted Comment has been missed.

   After approval, changes to scope, methodology, sample, sources or timetable are Changes (clause 6.3).

# FORM B: FINAL DELIVERABLE ACCEPTANCE FORM (D3 and D4 Materials)

## Guidance Notes for Form B

These notes summarise clauses 7.9 to 7.16 of the Agreement. If they conflict, the Agreement prevails.

1. **Review Period.** The Client has [NEGOTIABLE: 5] Business Days after the Delivery Date to review the Final Deliverable (clause 7.9). The Provider inserts the end date in Part B1 for convenience; clause 7.9 governs if that date is wrong.

2. **To accept.** Tick "Accepted" in Part B2, sign Part B5 and return the form.

3. **To reject.** Tick "Rejected" in Part B2 and complete Part B3, Section 1. The rejection must:
   - be received by the Provider **before the Review Period ends**;
   - identify each Non-Conformity in reasonable detail, with reference to the Acceptance Criterion not met; and
   - be emailed to the Provider's project email address **with a copy to its notice email address**. Both addresses are in Part B5 (clause 7.12).

4. **Valid ground for rejection.** The only valid ground is a **Non-Conformity**: a failure to conform in a material respect with the Deliverable's description in SOW section 5 or with the Approved Research Plan (clause 7.10).

5. **Grounds that are not valid (clause 7.11):**
   - (a) disagreement with findings, conclusions, estimates, opinions or recommendations reached in the Provider's professional judgement;
   - (b) findings that are commercially unwelcome or do not support the Client's preferred course of action;
   - (c) limitations in the available data that are disclosed in the Deliverable;
   - (d) issues caused by a Client Default, or by inaccurate or incomplete Client Materials;
   - (e) changes to the Client's requirements after the Approved Research Plan was approved;
   - (f) failure to reflect a Comment on an Interim Deliverable that the Provider was not required to adopt (clause 7.6); and
   - (g) minor errors, such as typographical or formatting errors. List these in Part B3, Section 2. They do not prevent Acceptance.

6. **Invalid rejections (clause 7.13).** If the Provider considers a rejection invalid, it will say so within [NEGOTIABLE: 2] Business Days, giving reasons. The Deliverable is then Accepted at the later of the end of the Review Period and [NEGOTIABLE: 2] Business Days after the Provider's notification, unless a valid rejection is delivered within that time.

7. **Deemed acceptance (clause 7.16).** A Final Deliverable is Accepted on the earliest of:
   - the Client's written confirmation;
   - the end of the Review Period, if no valid rejection has been received; and
   - use of the Deliverable in the Client's business, or its disclosure to a third party other than for review.

8. **Revisions (clauses 7.14 and 7.15).** One round of revisions is included. Valid Non-Conformities are corrected and the Deliverable is redelivered within [NEGOTIABLE: 5] Business Days, or a longer reasonable period. Re-review is limited to the previously notified Non-Conformities.

9. **Payment.** Acceptance of both D3 and the D4 Materials forms part of the Second Instalment Trigger (Annex 1, section 4).
