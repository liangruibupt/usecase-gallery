# LAWYER REVIEW REQUIRED CHECKLIST

## Consulting Services Agreement, SOW No. 1, Annex 1 (Payment Schedule) and Annex 2 (Deliverable Acceptance Form)

### Example Alpha Research Ltd (Provider) and Example Beta Commerce Ltd (Client)

> **INTERNAL: FOR THE PROVIDER AND ITS LEGAL ADVISERS ONLY.**
> **Purpose:** This checklist lists the points in the Provider's drafts that a qualified lawyer practising in England and Wales should review before the drafts are sent to the Client or signed. The drafts are for commercial negotiation and legal review. They are not legal advice.

**Priority key**

| Priority | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **High** | Resolve before the draft is sent to the Client |
| **Medium** | Resolve before signature |
| **Low** | Confirm when convenient |

---

## 1. Review Items

| # | Document and clause | Issue | Question for the lawyer | Priority | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Agt Parties, 9.6, 9.7, 12.6, 26.3; Annex 1 s.8 | The Client's jurisdiction of incorporation and place of establishment are unconfirmed. | Confirm the Client's status. Advise on: VAT; withholding; enforcing an English judgment in the Client's jurisdiction; whether a process agent is needed (26.3; delete if UK-incorporated); whether a parent company guarantee is advisable. | High | Open |
| L2 | Agt 9.6; Annex 1 s.8 | VAT treatment depends on the Provider's VAT registration and the Client's place of belonging. | Confirm the VAT treatment and invoice wording. Is the neutral drafting adequate? | High | Open |
| L3 | Agt 15.3–15.7 | The cap (100% of Fees paid and payable) and the exclusions must satisfy the reasonableness test under the Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977 (ss. 2(2), 3 and 11). | Is the cap reasonable for a £12,000 advisory engagement? | L4 | Agt 15.4(a) | The exclusion of losses from the "commercial outcome" of market-entry decisions might be read as excluding all liability for negligent advice. | Is it enforceable as drafted? Should the amount be justified as a mobilisation or reservation fee? | Medium | Open |
| L12 | Agt 10.3–10.4, 10.9 | Present assignment of future rights, conditional on payment of all Fees (including clause 19 sums); licence pending assignment; waiver of moral rights. | Is the assignment effective under s.91 of the Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988? | L13 | Agt 10.5–10.7 | Licence of embedded Background IP; retention of Research Data; Third-Party Materials. | Are the scope and restrictions adequate? Do the Provider's third-party data licences permit inclusion in Client-owned Deliverables? | Medium | Open |
| L14 | Agt 12; SOW 3.4, 10, CD10 | The Provider is independent controller for research participants. No Client personal data is supplied. Deliverables are anonymised. | L15 | Agt 12.6 | Possible restricted transfer if the Client is overseas. | Is any transfer mechanism needed, given that Deliverables are anonymised? | Low (Medium if the Client is overseas) | Open |

Is the boundary adequate? | Medium | Open |
| L17 | Agt 11.5–11.8; SOW s.12 | Non-reliance by third parties; consent conditions; the Client indemnity. | Are these enforceable and reasonable? Are a standard-form reliance letter and non-reliance letter needed before signature? | Medium | Open |
| L18 | Agt 9.7 | Withholding tax gross-up. | Is it necessary and appropriate given the Client's location? Delete if the Client is UK-established? | Low (High if the Client is overseas) | Open |

| L20 | Agt 14.3–14.4 | Re-performance as the "first" remedy; exclusion of implied terms other than reasonable care and skill. | Should re-performance be the sole remedy? Is the interaction with s.13 of the Supply of Goods and Services Act 1982 and with UCTA correct? | Medium | Open |
| L21 | Agt 2.3–2.4, 25.1, 25.9 | Order of precedence; entire agreement; exclusion of Client purchase order and portal terms. | Is the protection against battle-of-the-forms risk adequate, including portal click-through terms accepted after signature? | Low | Open |
