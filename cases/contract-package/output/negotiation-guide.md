## 2. Priorities

**Must-haves.** Escalate internally before conceding any of these.

1. Instalment 1 is received before fieldwork starts or any panel cost is committed (9.5).
2. The acceptance test is objective, with deemed acceptance on expiry of the Review Period. Disagreement with findings is never a ground for rejection (7.3, 7.4, 7.9(b)).
3. The liability cap does not exceed PI cover. Loss of profit is excluded, and so is loss arising from market-entry outcomes (15.3–15.5).
4. The Deliverables are not legal, tax, regulatory or financial advice, carry no guarantee of outcome, and may not be relied on by third parties (11.1–11.6).
5. The Provider keeps its Background IP, methodologies and Research Data (10.2, 10.5, 10.6).

**Important.** Concede only in exchange for something.

6. The Accepted D1 is the scope baseline (6.2–6.3).
7. Relief for Client delay, and the extended-delay payment route (5.4–5.6).
8. Instalment 1 is non-refundable on termination for convenience (19.2).
9. Payment terms of 14 days (9.3(a)).
10. Exclusion of the Client's purchase order and standard terms (25.9).

## 6. Red Flags in a Client Mark-up

- Payment linked to the Client's "satisfaction" or "approval in its sole discretion".
- Liquidated damages or service credits for late delivery, or time stated to be of the essence (contrary to 4.2).
- Uncapped Provider liability for negligence or confidentiality.
- A Provider indemnity for regulatory, legal or market-entry losses.
- Ownership of Background IP, templates, questionnaires or Research Data passing to the Client.
- A requirement for the Provider to act as processor without Article 28 terms, or the supply of customer lists (contrary to 12.4).
- A right of set-off, or payment withheld pending a purchase order (contrary to 9.4 and 9.12).
- Foreign governing law or foreign courts.
- Client purchase order or portal terms overriding the Agreement (contrary to 25.9).
- Deletion of the no-advice statement or the non-reliance wording (11.1, 11.5).

## 8. Talking Points

- **Payment:** "The fee is fixed and the schedule is balanced. Half up front to mobilise the team and fieldwork, and half when the final outputs are accepted. Deemed acceptance and the long-stop mean neither of us is left waiting."
- **Acceptance:** "You can always require us to fix anything that doesn't match what we agreed to deliver. Professional judgement can't be a ground for rejection, though. Otherwise the research would stop being independent."
- **Liability:** "The cap is tied to the fee and to our insurance, which is standard for fixed-fee research. We can't underwrite the commercial success of your UK launch, because that depends on many decisions outside our control."
- **Scope:** "The week-one research plan is your chance to shape the work. Once you approve it, we both have certainty about what will be delivered."
- **Client delay:** "The timetable depends on inputs and feedback from your side. The delay provisions simply stop a fixed fee from becoming open-ended."
