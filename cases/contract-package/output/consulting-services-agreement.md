2.1 This Agreement sets out the general terms on which the Provider will provide Services to the Client. Each SOW forms a separate contract that incorporates these terms.

2.2 A SOW is binding only when both parties have signed it. The Provider has no obligation to provide any services except under a signed SOW.

2.3 If there is any conflict or inconsistency between the documents forming the Agreement, they take precedence in this descending order:

(a) the clauses of this Agreement;

(b) the relevant SOW, excluding its annexes;

(c) the Payment Schedule;

(d) the Approved Research Plan; and

(e) any other document referred to in the SOW, including the forms in Annex 2.

2.4 A SOW may vary a specific clause of this Agreement for that SOW only. It does so only if it expressly identifies the clause being varied and states that the variation is intended to take precedence.

## 3. Commencement and Duration

3.1 This Agreement takes effect on the date on which both parties have signed it (the **"Effective Date"**). It continues until terminated under clause 18.
