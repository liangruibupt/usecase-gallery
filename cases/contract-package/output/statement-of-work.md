## 3. Services in Scope

The Provider shall perform the following Services.

### 3.1 Inception

A remote kick-off briefing of up to [NEGOTIABLE: 1.5] hours with the Client's project contact and nominated stakeholders. The briefing will confirm objectives and priorities and identify the Client Materials to be supplied.

### 3.2 Research planning (D1)

Preparation and delivery of the Initial research plan described in section 5 and in clause 6.1 of the Agreement. Once Accepted, D1 becomes the Approved Research Plan and the baseline for D2 to D4 (clause 6.2 of the Agreement).

### 3.3 Desk research

Research using publicly available sources and sources licensed to the Provider, covering:

- market size and structure;
- market trends and drivers;
- competitors;
- distribution channels;
- pricing benchmarks; and
- the main regulatory themes relevant to market entry. These themes are identified for information only and are not advised on (clause 11.1 of the Agreement).

Desk research may begin before D1 is Accepted (clause 6.4 of the Agreement).

## 4. Out of Scope

The following are excluded from the Services and the fixed fee. Any of them may be requested as a Change under clause 8 of the Agreement.

(a) Legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, financial, investment or other professional advice. This includes advice on company formation, licensing, product compliance, employment and data protection (clause 11.1 of the Agreement).

(b) Implementation of market entry. This includes partner or distributor search and introductions, recruitment, marketing execution and sales activity.

(c) Primary research beyond the method, volume and participant profile in the Approved Research Plan.

(d) Independent verification of Client Materials or Third-Party Materials (clause 11.3 of the Agreement).

(e) More than one round of revisions for each Deliverable (clause 7.6 of the Agreement).

(f) More than one presentation, any in-person presentation, and any presentation to persons outside the Client's Group.

(g) Translation of Deliverables into any language other than English.

(h) Support after the D4 Presentation, including updating Deliverables for later market developments.

(i) Processing of personal data supplied by the Client, other than business contact details (clause 12.4 of the Agreement).
