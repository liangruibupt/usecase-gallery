## 4. Second Instalment Trigger

4.1 The **Second Instalment Trigger** is the Payment Trigger for Instalment 2. It occurs on the earliest of the following.

| Route | Condition | Trigger date | Agreement reference |
|---|---|---|---|
| (a) Completion | D3 and the D4 Materials have both been Accepted, and the D4 Presentation has been given | The date on which the last of these occurs | 7.16, 7.17(b) |
| (b) Presentation long-stop | D3 and the D4 Materials have both been Accepted. The D4 Presentation has not been given by the Long-Stop Date, and there has been no Provider Presentation Failure. | The Long-Stop Date | 7.17(e) |
| (c) Extended Client delay | The Provider has given notice under clause 5.5(b) of the Agreement | The date of that notice | 5.5(b), 5.6 |

4.2 **Long-Stop Date.** For the purposes of clause 7.17 of the Agreement, the **Long-Stop Date** is the date falling [NEGOTIABLE: 10] Business Days after the later of:

(a) the Delivery Date of the version of D3 that is Accepted; and

(b) the Delivery Date of the version of the D4 Materials that is Accepted.

4.3 **Offered dates.** Once the D4 Materials have been delivered, the Provider will offer at least [NEGOTIABLE: 3] Business Days for the D4 Presentation, each falling on or before the Long-Stop Date (clause 7.17(c)). The record in Form C of Annex 2 may be used as evidence of the dates offered.

4.4 **Presentation after the Long-Stop Date.** If route (b) applies, the Provider will still give the D4 Presentation on a mutually agreed date within [NEGOTIABLE: 30] days after the Long-Stop Date. After that period, the obligation lapses (clause 7.17(f)). This does not affect the Payment Trigger, the Invoice Date or the Due Date of Instalment 2.

4.5 **Provider Presentation Failure.** If there has been a Provider Presentation Failure (clause 7.17(d)), route (b) does not apply. Route (a) then applies once the D4 Presentation is given (clause 7.17(g)).

4.6 **Acceptance includes deemed acceptance.** In each route, "Accepted" includes deemed Acceptance under clauses 7.13 and 7.16 of the Agreement. In particular, a Final Deliverable is Accepted at the end of its Review Period if no rejection notice complying with clause 7.12 has been received by then.

4.7 **Effect of a valid rejection.** A valid rejection of D3 or the D4 Materials postpones routes (a) and (b) until the redelivered Final Deliverable is Accepted (clauses 7.14 and 7.15). It does not affect route (c).

4.8 **Interim Deliverables.** Comments on D1 or D2, and the issue of the Finalised Research Plan, do not affect the Second Instalment Trigger.
